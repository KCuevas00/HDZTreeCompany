Certified Arborist Health & Risk Assessments

Not every troubled tree needs to be cut down. Our certified arborist conducts thorough diagnostics on trunk acoustics, fungal conks, root collar rot, pest infestations (such as emerald ash borer), and soil compaction. We provide honest, actionable assessments aimed at preserving mature shade trees whenever safe.

  • Decay & trunk hollow evaluation
  • Fungal & pest diagnosis
  • Storm failure risk rating
  • Preservation vs. removal plan
Watch out for: Mushroom clusters at the base of the trunk, premature autumn defoliation, cracked main trunk crotches, or sawdust frass from boring insects.

Deep Stump Grinding & Surface Root Eradication

Don't leave unsightly, hazardous tree stumps rotting in your yard. Our stump grinders chew stumps down well below ground level, addressing surface roots along the way. We leave the area leveled and ready for topsoil, grass seed, sod, or landscaping.

  • Grinding well below grade
  • Surface root chasing & removal
  • Compact machines for gated yards
  • Mulch backfill or haul away
Why grind stumps? Old stumps harbor subterranean termites, carpenter ants, and honeydew fungi, while creating dangerous trip hazards that damage lawnmower blades.

Frequently Asked Tree Questions

Common questions Aurora and Fox Valley homeowners ask before hiring a certified tree service.

Do I need a city permit to remove a tree on my property?

Permit requirements vary by municipality and can depend on the tree's location—for example, trees on public parkways are typically treated differently than trees on private residential property. We're happy to help you figure out what applies to your situation during your on-site consultation, but you should always confirm requirements with your local municipality before work begins.

How are tree removal and pruning costs calculated?

Pricing is determined by tree height and diameter, proximity to homes, power lines, and fences, canopy deadwood risk, and equipment accessibility. We provide clear, upfront written estimates before any work begins so there are no surprises.

Will heavy tree equipment damage my lawn, patio, or driveway?

We take extra precautions to protect your lawn, driveways, and landscaping, planning access routes carefully and using ground protection mats whenever needed.

Can HDZ help with homeowner insurance claims after storm damage?

We can provide photos and an itemized invoice for the work we perform, which can be useful if you're filing a claim after a storm damages your home, garage, fence, or driveway. Be sure to check with your insurance provider about their specific documentation requirements.

What happens to all the wood and branches cut down?

Our standard service includes chipping and hauling away branches, logs, and debris. If you'd like to keep firewood logs or mulch for garden beds instead, just let us know and we'll work that out with you.
