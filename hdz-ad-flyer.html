💡 Design Guide & Updated Layout Notes

Solid colors ensure the jagged paper trim blends seamlessly with the background, while the 4-photo Before & After grid displays dramatic tree removal transformations:

🎨 Solid Colors (Seamless Trim)
• Green Canvas: Single solid deep forest pine (#153C1F) matching torn SVG edges exactly.
• Kraft Paper: Single solid kraft tone (#E8DAC4) across top header and bottom footer.
• Seamless: Zero gradient banding, radial glare, or shading seam breaks.
📷 4-Photo Before & After Grid
• Plain Text: Plain un-fussy BEFORE and AFTER labels directly on the photos (no pill boxes or borders).
• Grayscale Befores: Subtle 45% desaturation on before shots to emphasize the vibrant green after results.
• Doubled Separation: Generous 48px vertical gap clearly distinguishing Pair 1 from Pair 2.
✨ Cleaned Up Elements
• Services Spacing: Doubled empty line spacing (50px gap) cleanly separating each core tree service.
• Trust Line: Locked to 1 single horizontal line with white-space: nowrap (no wrapping).
• 24/7 Response: Clean glowing lightning bolt icon + bold text, completely unbordered.
📐 Interactive Controls
• Swap Before/Afters: Cycle through other transformation pairs from your job gallery.
• Switch Theme: Switch between 4 curated solid contractor palettes.
• High-Res PNG: 1-click retina image download ready for social media and print.
